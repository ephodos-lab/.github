Ephodos Lab builds open-source tools for automated mathematical research. They cover the whole path of a research project: choosing a problem worth working on, proving results with AI agents whose every claim is checked, and writing the results up as a paper.

## Projects

| Project | Stage | What it does | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **topic-gate** | Problem selection | Agents propose research problems. Fresh reviewer agents try to rule each one out, and only problems that survive reach a human for approval. | Coming soon |
| **Danus** | Research | A main agent directs a team of proving agents. A fresh verifier checks every claim before it enters a shared fact graph. Our fork of [frenzymath/Danus](https://github.com/frenzymath/Danus) (Apache-2.0). | Coming soon |
| **mathgalley** | Writing | Turns notes, proofs, code and existing manuscripts into a paper, then reviews it before submission. | In development |
| Integrated platform | All three | Runs the three stages as one pipeline. | Planned |

Repositories appear on this page as they are released.

## How we work

- **Every stage ends in an independent review.** The reviewer sees the work itself, not the author's account of it. Work that fails review does not move on.
- **Exploration is free, proof is strict.** Agents may try anything, but a result counts only after a verifier accepts it.
- **We say how results were made.** Papers produced with these tools state plainly what AI contributed.

## Why "Ephodos"

*Ephodos* (ἔφοδος, "the method") is the title of a letter from Archimedes to Eratosthenes. In it Archimedes explains how he found results such as the volume of the sphere by weighing shapes against each other on a lever, and why he still proved them afterwards by rigorous geometry. He wrote the method down in the hope that others would use it to find theorems he had not thought of. Our logo is that balance: a circle and a square of equal area, weighed on a lever.

### 工作方式

- **每个阶段结束都有独立审查。** 审查者只看工作本身，不看作者的说明；没通过审查的工作不进入下一阶段。
- **探索不设限，证明要严格。** agent 可以随意尝试，但结论要经 verifier 接受才算数。
- **说明结果是怎么来的。** 用这些工具做出的论文会如实写明 AI 做了什么。

### 名字的来历

*Ephodos*（ἔφοδος，意为「方法」）是阿基米德写给埃拉托色尼的一封信的标题。阿基米德在信里讲，他先在杠杆上称量图形，由此发现球的体积等结果，之后仍用严格的几何证明它们。他把这个方法写下来，是希望别人能用它发现他自己还没想到的定理。我们的标志就是这架杠杆：一个圆和一个面积相等的正方形。
